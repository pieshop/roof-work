# Roofing Company Research

## Short View

Of the three, **Brandon Roofing Ltd looks strongest on paper for this specific job**, followed by **Resilient Roofing & Property Maintenance Ltd**. **Roofers of Surrey may still be fine**, but fewer independent verification signals were found, so they need more due diligence before appointment.

## 1. Brandon Roofing Ltd

### Observed

- Website says they specialise in new roofs, re-roofs, flat roofs, slate/tile, fascia/soffits/guttering, and are based in Epsom. Source: <https://www.brandonroofing.co.uk/>
- Checkatrade profile shows `10/10`, `144 reviews`, member since `March 2014`, VAT registered, and services including pitched roofing, slate/tiled roofing, roof replacement, leadworks, and chimney repairs/general roofing. Source: <https://www.checkatrade.com/trades/brandonroofing>
- Checkatrade has relevant review examples for full roof replacement and chimney stack work.
- Companies House shows `BRANDON ROOFING LTD`, company number `09516241`, active, incorporated `28 March 2015`, SIC `43910 - Roofing activities`. Source: <https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/09516241>

### Assessment

- Best match for this job on paper.
- Local to Epsom/Surrey.
- Long trading history and substantial Checkatrade history.
- Relevant experience appears to include roof replacement, slate/tile, leadwork, and chimney work.

### Questions To Ask

- Will they personally handle the gable masonry, wall ties, and purlin bearing/support, or bring in a masonry/structural specialist?
- Can they provide a written method for the chimney reduction and roof infill?
- What guarantee applies to roof work versus masonry/wall tie work?

## 2. Resilient Roofing & Property Maintenance Ltd

### Observed

- Website lists roofing services, roof repairs, lead work, chimney repairs/work, repointing, gable ends, guttering, drainage, and roof surveys. Source: <https://resilientroofingltd.com/contact/>
- Website gives address `31 Devonshire Road, Colliers Wood, London SW19 2EJ`, phone, email, and company number `12597379`.
- FMB profile lists them as a roofer/carpenter and joiner, with roofing general, slate/tiled roofing, leadworks, and roofing repairs. Source: <https://www.fmb.org.uk/builder/resilient-roofing-property-maintenance-ltd.html>
- Companies House confirms `RESILIENT ROOFING & PROPERTY MAINTENANCE LTD`, company number `12597379`, active, incorporated `12 May 2020`, SIC `43910 - Roofing activities`. Source: <https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12597379>
- Checkatrade profile shows `10/10`, `91 reviews`, but also says: “You cannot request a job with this trade through Checkatrade” because they are “not a Checkatrade Tradesperson”. Source: <https://www.checkatrade.com/trades/resilientroofing>

### Assessment

- Good fit on stated services: they explicitly mention chimney, gable ends, repointing, roof repairs, leadwork.
- FMB listing is a positive signal.
- Slight caution: Checkatrade reviews are visible, but Checkatrade states they are not currently a Checkatrade Tradesperson, so do not treat that as current platform membership.

### Questions To Ask

- Are they currently FMB members and can they provide their FMB membership details?
- Can they provide a current public liability insurance certificate?
- Who will undertake the structural masonry and wall tie work?
- Do they have recent examples of gable/chimney reduction jobs, ideally with references?

## 3. Roofers Of Surrey

### Observed

- Website lists full re-roofs, loft conversions, extension roofs, flat roofs, roof repairs, fascia/guttering, and gutter cleaning. Source: <https://www.roofers-of-surrey.com/>
- Website states they are fully insured and covered by Public Liability Insurance.
- Website gives Epsom address `28 Tonstall Road, Epsom, Surrey, KT19 9DP`, phone numbers, and email.
- Website has testimonials and project images.
- No clear Companies House match for “Roofers of Surrey” was found from the public search result checked. Source searched: <https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/search?q=Roofers%20of%20Surrey>

### Assessment

- They are local and may be suitable for inspection/quote.
- Compared with Brandon and Resilient, fewer third-party verification signals were found from the sources checked.
- Their website reads more like a small/local operation; that is not necessarily a problem, but for this job be stricter on evidence of insurance, structural competence, and references.

### Questions To Ask

- What is the legal trading entity name?
- Are they a limited company or sole trader?
- Can they provide a public liability insurance certificate?
- Can they provide references/photos for chimney reduction, gable masonry, wall tie, or purlin support work?
- Who handles structural masonry if required?

## Recommendation

Let all three inspect and quote, but compare them on:

- Whether they understand this as **chimney + roof + structural gable masonry**, not just roofing.
- Whether the quote is itemised.
- Whether they explicitly address wall ties and purlin bearing/support.
- Whether they can evidence insurance and relevant previous jobs.
- Whether they flag planning/building control rather than ignoring it.

On paper, the strongest order is:

1. **Brandon Roofing Ltd**
2. **Resilient Roofing & Property Maintenance Ltd**
3. **Roofers of Surrey**, pending due diligence
