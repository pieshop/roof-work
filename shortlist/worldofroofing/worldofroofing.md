#### Why shortlisted
Very local Epsom contractor with a range of roofing and chimney services relevant to the proposed work.

Their advertised services specifically include work to structurally unsound chimneys, along with brickwork, repointing, capping, leadwork and parapet walls. This makes them potentially suitable for the chimney, slate roof and some of the high-level masonry elements.

The main question is whether they have the capability to take responsibility for the structural gable/flank wall, wall-tie and purlin-bearing works. No evidence has yet been identified that they routinely undertake these elements or coordinate structural specialists.

They also have less independently verifiable trade accreditation than the other shortlisted contractors, with no NFRC membership identified. For that reason they are currently a reserve candidate rather than a first-choice contact.

#### Potential questions / checks
- Will they act as principal contractor for the complete package?
- Can they undertake the structural gable/flank wall repairs?
- Can they undertake or arrange the additional wall-tie work?
- Can they undertake the purlin bearing/support repairs?
- Will they work to the structural engineer's recommendations?
- Will they liaise with the structural engineer if required?
- Is Building Control involvement required?
- What guarantees are provided for each part of the work?
- Is scaffolding arranged and managed by them?
- Can existing slates be reused where appropriate?
