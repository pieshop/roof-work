Can you confirm the trading history, which entity would contract for this job, and who would issue and honour the guarantee?

## Structural Responsibility Checks

- Confirm whether Roofcraft will act as principal contractor for the whole package.
- Ask which elements Roofcraft will do directly.
- Ask which elements will be subcontracted.
- Ask who is responsible for subcontractor workmanship, insurance, programme and defects.
- Ask whether Roofcraft will work directly from the White & Lloyd structural recommendations.
- Ask whether further structural engineer input is required before work starts.
- Ask whether Building Control involvement is required.
- Ask whether planning permission or party-wall issues are relevant to chimney reduction/removal.

## Roof Material Checks

- Ask for the exact proposed roof covering: natural slate, fibre-cement slate, concrete tile or other.
- Ask for manufacturer, model/product, colour, size and expected lifespan.
- If concrete tiles are proposed, ask for written confirmation that the existing roof structure is suitable for the increased dead load.
- Given the existing purlin/gable issues, get White & Lloyd or another structural engineer to confirm concrete tile loading if there is any doubt.
- Ask why Ben recommends against reusing the existing slates.
- Ask whether any original slates can sensibly be retained for repairs to smaller areas, outbuildings or future matching.

## Guarantees And Insurance

- Ask for current public liability insurance certificate.
- Ask for employer's liability insurance if employees or labourers will be on site.
- Confirm cover levels and expiry dates.
- Confirm whether the roofing guarantee is Roofcraft workmanship only or insurance-backed.
- Ask whether any CORC insurance-backed guarantee is available for this job.
- Ask whether any TrustATrader or Checkatrade guarantee applies, and what needs registering before work starts.
- Ask what guarantee applies separately to roof covering, leadwork, chimney work, structural repairs, wall ties, purlin work and leak patching.
- Ask what happens if the limited company stops trading during the guarantee period.

## References And Previous Work

- Ask for at least two recent full reroof references.
- Prioritise old houses, slate roofs, chimney work or jobs where hidden defects were found after stripping.
- Ask specifically for the nearby reroof Ben mentioned.
- If possible, inspect the nearby completed roof from the street or with the homeowner's permission.
- Ask references whether the final cost matched the quote.
- Ask references whether the job stayed on schedule.
- Ask references how Ben handled communication, disruption, scaffold, tidiness, snagging and aftercare.
- Ask references whether there have been leaks or defects since completion.

## Payment And Contract Terms

- Avoid large upfront payments.
- Prefer staged payments tied to clear milestones: scaffold/materials, watertight stage, completion and snagging.
- Confirm start date, expected duration and working hours.
- Confirm how weather delays are handled.
- Confirm who owns materials delivered to site before installation.
- Confirm cancellation terms.
- Confirm whether quote is fixed-price or estimate.
- Confirm how variations will be priced and approved before work proceeds.

## Independent Second Opinion

- Get at least one independent assessment before committing if practical.
- Ask another credible contractor to assess repair versus complete replacement.
- Ask whether slate reuse is sensible.
- Ask whether natural slate, fibre-cement slate or concrete tile is most appropriate.
- Ask specifically about structural implications of changing from slate to concrete tile.
- South Thames Roofing, Jack Auletta, Resilient Roofing or Roofers of Surrey may be useful comparators.

## Decision Rule

Do not choose Roofcraft solely because Ben made a good impression or offered a quick start. Choose them only if the written quote clearly covers the full scope, structural responsibility is explicit, guarantees and insurance are clear, references check out, and at least one independent view does not materially contradict the proposed approach.
