Structural:

The White & Lloyd structural report recommends repairs to the bulging gable/flank wall, additional wall ties and support to the front purlin. If that work turns out to be needed once the roof is off, would you carry it out yourselves, and how would it be priced and agreed?
I have allowed for purlin supports within the quotation.  If we needed to add wall ties, once we are aware of the situation then we would be looking at around £350 + VAT to supply and install.  If anything alarming were to show up then we would down tools and stop until the structural engineer fully assessed the situation.
The quote includes 4x2 timber supports to the loft where necessary. Is that intended to address the purlin bearing/support issue in the structural report?
Yes, that’s correct.
The Level 2 survey noted water staining under the ridge. If you find any defective rafters or other timbers once the roof is stripped, how would that be priced and agreed?
I would be very surprised if there were any rotten timbers.  When timbers rot there are usually visible signs as the rafters bow/drop and that impacts how the slates sit.  The ridge line was visibly level at the time of the visit so I am confident that it will be sound under there.  If there is damage then we would need to look at the damage, assess what needs to be done and agree a price before we continue.  However I think that this is highly unlikely.
Do you expect any of the structural work to need a structural engineer, Building Control or a specialist subcontractor? If so, would you arrange that and is it covered in your price?
Only if there are substantial issues which are evident, once the structure is exposed.  Again, I do not believe that there will be anything of large scale.
The structural report states that the chimney reduction and high-level gable brickwork are likely to require a planning application. Do you agree?
Chimney reductions do not require planning, unless you are in a listed building or in a conservation area.  In normal residential homes, both aspects fall under permitted development.
If structural work is needed once the roof is stripped, how would the roof be protected while that is being resolved?
We will leave it felted and battened which is not 100% watertight but isn’t far off!
