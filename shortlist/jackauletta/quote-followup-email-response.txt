Roofing: (Matthew)

10. Can you confirm the natural slate supplier/origin, grade, warranty and expected lifespan? The proposed slate is a Pizzaro Spanish natural slate, 250mmx500mm, supplied by Slatescapes.
The slate is a First Grade product, meaning the slates require less grading and sorting due to their consistency and quality.
The manufacturers/suppliers warranty is 100 years. Natural slate is a long life roofing material and when correctly installed and maintained is expected to provide a service life extending for many decades. The 100 year warranty should not be interpreted as a definitive limit on the slates serviceable lifespan.
11. How will the verges be finished? The verges will be finished in a similar fashion to the existiung roof, maintaining the existing appearance. Full slates and slate and a half slates will be alternated up the verge to maintain the required half bond between courses. The slates will also be finished with the required 2 inch oversail beyond the existing barge boards. This oversail ensures rainwater discharged clear of the existing barge boards rather than running back onto and against the timber work.
12. The quote reuses the existing main ridge tiles bedded in mortar, whereas the rear ridge is dry-fixed. Why the difference, and would a dry-fix main ridge be an option? The existing main ridge tiles are a specific interlocking ridge profile, which we intend to cafefully remove and re use as part of the re roof. Due to the particular profile of the ridge tiles, a dry fix system is not considered suitable for the re use. The main roof ridge is shared with the adjoining property so reusing the ridge tiles and installing with the current mortar bed appearance will allow us to tie in the new roof into the neighbouring roof appropriately. We will still provide ventilation to the main roof, fascia vents will be installed at the roofline and inline slate vents will be installed near the ridge to assist the airflow through the roof space. 
The rear roof is being treated differently as there are no neighbouring properties to tie into and the proposed new ridges tiles are a specific profile which is compatible with a dry fix system. We will therefore install a dry fix system which will require high level ventilation and assist in creating a continuous air flow through the loft space.
