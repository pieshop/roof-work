## Follow-Up Questions Before Decision

### Ask South Thames

Structural specification:

- The White & Lloyd structural report recommends repairs to the bulging gable/flank wall, additional wall ties and support to the front purlin. If that work turns out to be needed once the roof is off, would you carry it out yourselves, and how would it be priced and agreed?
- The quote includes 4x2 timber supports to the loft where necessary. Is that intended to address the purlin bearing/support issue in the structural report?
- The Level 2 survey noted water staining under the ridge. If you find any defective rafters or other timbers once the roof is stripped, how would that be priced and agreed?
- Do you expect any of the structural work to need a structural engineer, Building Control or a specialist subcontractor? If so, would you arrange that and is it covered in your price?
- The structural report states that the chimney reduction and high-level gable brickwork are likely to require a planning application. Do you agree?
- If structural work is needed once the roof is stripped, how would the roof be protected while that is being resolved?

Roofing specification:

- Could you let me know the proposed natural slate supplier/origin and warranty?
- How will the verges be finished?
- The quote doesn't mention the rear valley or the junction with the neighbour's roof. What is included for those?
- The leadwork and repointing refer to the chimney stack. Does that cover both retained stacks, the front one and the one on the rear addition, and is any work to pots or flaunching included?
- As part of the works, will you check whether the existing gutters, downpipes, outlets and drainage are contributing to water discharge against the flank wall, and is any correction included in the quote?
- I'm planning to have the loft insulation dealt with separately by the loft boarding contractor. Would taking the insulation top-up out of your scope affect the price or the LABC certificate?
- Is the slate roof specification compatible with a later solar PV installation, and would solar mounting affect the roof guarantee?

Miscellaneous:

- The quotation is dated 15th September 2027. I assume that should read 2026; could you confirm?
- Could you send a copy of the 15-year guarantee terms, including the insurance-backed element?
- When could you realistically start, and roughly how long would the works take?
- If we go ahead, could you send copies of your public liability and employer's liability insurance certificates before work starts?

Answered by quote: payment is due on completion; LABC certificate is included; Grade A natural slate at 500x250mm; mechanically dry-fixed ridge; scaffold all around the property; skips and waste removal included.
