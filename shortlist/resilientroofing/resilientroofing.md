#### Why shortlisted
Strong candidate for the roofing, chimney and high-level gable elements of the proposed work.

TrustMark specifically lists chimney repairs, flashing, pitched roofs, re-roofing, repairs and slate work. Its carpentry registration also includes timber frames, which may be relevant to the roof/purlin work.

The company's own advertised services include chimney work and gable-end work, and it states that a Chartered Architectural Technologist oversees its construction and roofing work.

The company covers Surrey as well as South London.

The main area to establish is whether it can take responsibility for the structural gable/flank wall repairs, additional wall ties and purlin-bearing/support work. These structural masonry and wall-tie elements are not explicitly included in its TrustMark registered trades.

#### Potential questions / checks
- Will they act as principal contractor for the complete package?
- Can they undertake the structural gable/flank wall repairs?
- Can they undertake or arrange the additional wall-tie work?
- Can they repair/reinstate the purlin bearing/support?
- Can they undertake the chimney reduction/removal and subsequent roof making-good?
- Will they work to the structural engineer's recommendations?
- What role would their Chartered Architectural Technologist have in the project?
- Will they liaise with the structural engineer if further input is required?
- Is Building Control involvement required?
- Is scaffolding arranged and managed by them?
- What guarantees are provided for the different elements of the work?
- Can existing slates be reused where appropriate?
