# Roof Work

Research, planning notes, contractor correspondence and quote-check material for roof, chimney and structural repairs at 66 Lower Court Road, Epsom.

## Key Documents

- `job-description.txt` - authoritative scope to inspect and quote.
- `working-context.md` - current project state, contractor ranking and immediate next steps.
- `questions.md` - questions for contractor visits and quote checks.
- `costs-estimate.md` - working budget assumptions and repair/replacement scenarios.
- `solar-panel.md` - solar PV considerations for reroof timing, covering choice and future installation.
- `00.reports/` - Level 2 survey and White & Lloyd structural assessment.

## Main Folders

- `shortlist/` - contractor research, contact history, validation notes and current ranking.
- `quotes/` - contractor quote PDFs and estimates received.
- `info/` - stable background summaries for structural issues, roof options and loft access.
- `phases/` - dated phase notes and historical context.
- `emergency-repair/` - active leak notes, emergency roofer shortlist and repair brief.
- `00.archived/` - older source notes retained for reference.

## Current Working View

The project is likely to involve roof replacement or major roof works, chimney reduction/removal, high-level gable/flank wall repairs, wall ties, purlin bearing/support, scaffolding, rainwater goods and possible solar PV coordination. Preference is for one principal contractor who can take responsibility for the complete package, either directly or by managing suitable specialists.

Treat `job-description.txt` as the primary scope reference when assessing contractor responses or quotations.
