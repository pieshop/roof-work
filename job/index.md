## Before Work Starts

- [ ] Review completed agreement / Schedule 1 Agreement.
- [ ] Review milestone payment schedule and check it matches the no-pre-start-deposit position.
- [ ] Speak to and, if suitable, appoint the structural designer / engineer.
- [ ] Confirm the designer's role: repair design/specification, inspection/sign-off, and whether output is suitable for Building Control.
- [ ] Confirm the designer has appropriate PI cover.
- [ ] Confirm Building Control route: whether required, who applies, who pays, and what certificate/sign-off is expected.
- [ ] Confirm base guarantee: duration, what it covers, exclusions, and whether workmanship/roof/structural works are treated differently.
- [ ] Decide whether to pursue TrustMark insurance-backed guarantee after cost/terms are known.
- [ ] Check whether the agreement covers scaffold delay/extension costs if structural design, Building Control, neighbour/party-wall issues or variations extend the programme beyond 6-8 weeks.
- [ ] Confirm welfare/toilet and hot/cold water arrangement.
- [ ] Confirm neighbour roof/bonding gutter protection and any pre-start internal photos below the works.
- [ ] Confirm start date, scaffold date and expected sequence.
- [ ] Clarify planning/permitted development position for chimney reduction and high-level gable brickwork if not already resolved.

## During Works

- [ ] Record opened-up findings before approving variations.
- [ ] Ensure any variation is described and priced before work proceeds, except genuine emergency weatherproofing/safety work.
- [ ] Confirm whether the gable wall rebuild allowance is being used, reduced or varied once the structure is exposed.
- [ ] Confirm treatment of any extra retained-wall ties, inner-leaf infill or purlin bearing/support work.
- [ ] Keep photos of key stages: scaffold/weather lid, stripped roof, exposed gable/purlin area, structural repairs, membrane/battens, leadwork, final covering.
- [ ] Keep copies of structural designer notes, Building Control correspondence and contractor variation approvals.
- [ ] Track invoices against the milestone schedule before paying.

## Completion / Handover

- [ ] Obtain final invoice and payment record.
- [ ] Obtain written guarantee document.
- [ ] Obtain Building Control / certification evidence if applicable.
- [ ] Obtain structural designer sign-off or inspection note if applicable.
- [ ] Obtain final photos or take own record photos.
- [ ] Record any exclusions, deferred works or snagging items.
- [ ] Update roof/solar/loft notes if the finished roof affects future solar PV or loft boarding.
