## Questions For Stephen Baker / Site Visit

### Scope And Principal Contractor Role

> If you do quote, could you confirm whether South Thames Roofing Ltd would act as principal contractor for the full roof/chimney/gable/wall-tie/purlin package, or whether some elements would need separate specialists?

Ask:

- Which parts would South Thames do directly?
- Which parts would be subcontracted?
- Who would be responsible for subcontractor workmanship, insurance, programme and defects?
- Who would be the day-to-day site/project lead?
- Would South Thames arrange and manage scaffolding?

### Structural Work

- Can you undertake the gable/flank wall masonry repairs directly?
- Can you undertake or arrange the wall-tie work?
- Can you reinstate/provide proper purlin bearing/support?
- Will the work follow the White & Lloyd structural recommendations?
- Do you think further structural engineer input is required?
- Is Building Control involvement required?
- Is planning permission or party-wall input relevant to the chimney reduction/removal?

### Roof Assessment

- Do you recommend repairing/overhauling the existing slate roof, stripping and re-slating, or full replacement?
- Why?
- Is there evidence of nail sickness or failing fixings?
- Are the existing slates worth retaining or reusing?
- If stripping is recommended, what percentage of slate is likely reusable?
- What membrane, battens and ventilation details would you propose?
- Would any timber repairs be included or provisional?

### Material Choice

- Would you recommend natural slate, reclaimed slate, fibre-cement slate, concrete tile or another covering?
- If concrete tile is proposed, what are the structural loading implications compared with the existing slate roof?
- Given the purlin/gable issues, should a structural engineer confirm suitability before changing to concrete?
- What exact product and manufacturer would be used?

### Quote Format

- Please break the quote down by main work area where practical.
- Confirm scaffold/access.
- Confirm chimney reduction/removal.
- Confirm roof making-good or reroofing scope.
- Confirm gable/flank masonry repairs.
- Confirm wall-tie works.
- Confirm purlin bearing/support works.
- Confirm flashings/ridge/junction repairs.
- Confirm rainwater goods/drainage corrections.
- Confirm waste removal.
- Confirm VAT.
- Confirm exclusions, assumptions and provisional sums.

### Guarantees And Insurance

- Please provide current public liability insurance.
- Please provide employer's liability insurance if relevant.
- Confirm cover levels and expiry dates.
- What guarantee would apply to roofing work?
- What guarantee would apply to chimney work?
- What guarantee would apply to structural masonry, wall ties and purlin support?
- Are any guarantees insurance-backed?
- Can any NFRC, CORC, Competent Roofer, TrustMark or manufacturer guarantee/certification apply to this job?
- Are any manufacturer warranties available for roof covering, membrane or other products?

### References

- Can you provide references for recent whole-roof or major roof projects?
- Can you provide references for older-house or slate-roof projects?
- Can you provide examples involving chimney reduction/removal or structural masonry?
- Are there comparable completed jobs nearby that can be viewed externally?
