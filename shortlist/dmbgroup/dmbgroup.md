#### Why shortlisted
Potentially an excellent match for the complete package because DMB is a multi-discipline building contractor rather than solely a roofing contractor.

Their own website specifically describes structural works and alterations, steel installation and full project management, including coordination of trades. This is particularly relevant because the proposed work crosses structural masonry, roofing and chimney repairs and may require specialist subcontractors.

Their FMB listing is even more directly relevant to this project, listing structural repairs and alterations, wall-tie replacement, structural steelwork, brickwork, repointing and carpentry alongside slate/tiled roofing, chimney work, leadwork, guttering and scaffolding.

This combination closely matches the structural engineer's recommendations concerning the bulging gable/flank wall, additional wall ties and purlin bearing/support, while potentially allowing the chimney and roofing work to remain under a single principal contractor.

The main question is whether a relatively contained roof/chimney/gable repair project in Epsom is the type and size of project they are interested in undertaking.

#### Potential questions / checks
- Will they take responsibility for the complete package?
- Can they undertake the structural gable/flank wall repairs directly?
- Can they undertake the additional wall-tie work directly?
- Can they repair/reinstate the purlin bearing/support?
- Can they undertake the chimney reduction and subsequent slate roof making-good?
- Will they work to the existing structural engineer's recommendations?
- Will they liaise with the structural engineer if further input is required?
- Is Building Control involvement required?
- Is scaffolding arranged and managed by them?
- What guarantees are provided for the different elements of the work?
- Can existing slates be reused where appropriate?
