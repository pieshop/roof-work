# Structural Summary

Use `job-description.txt` as the authoritative contractor scope and the White & Lloyd structural assessment as the structural authority. This file is a short working summary for conversations and quote checks.

## Main Area

The main structural work concerns the rear flank chimney and high-level gable/flank wall.

The rear chimney is leaning significantly inward and the structural engineer has recommended reducing/removing it.

The high-level gable/flank wall is bulging and has defective or missing masonry near roof level.

The structural report also recommends:

- Additional wall ties
- Repair/stabilisation of the affected masonry
- Reinstatement of proper support to a roof purlin where masonry has come away from its bearing

The intention is to repair and stabilise this whole area in accordance with the White & Lloyd recommendations.
