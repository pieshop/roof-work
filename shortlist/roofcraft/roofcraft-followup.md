## Questions For Ben

### Trading History / Contracting Entity

> I noticed CORC, TrustATrader and Checkatrade show Roofcraft trading/listed since around 2014/2015, while Companies House shows Roofcraft of Surrey & London Ltd incorporated in January 2024. Can you confirm whether you previously traded as a sole trader before incorporating, and whether the quote, invoice and guarantee for this job would be issued by the limited company?

Also ask:

- What exact legal name will appear on the quote and invoice?
- Is the trading name `Roof Craft of Surrey & London`, `Roofcraft of Surrey & London`, or `Roofcraft of Surrey & London Ltd`?
- If the limited company gives the guarantee, what happens if the company stops trading during the guarantee period?

### Quote #341 / Scope

> Thanks for sending quote #341. Could you please confirm the points below so I can compare it properly against the structural report and job description?

Confirm the quote covers:

- Scaffold/access
- Full strip or repair scope
- Waste removal
- Membrane
- Battens
- Roof covering supply and installation
- Ridge, verge, abutments and flashings
- Leadwork specification
- Chimney reduction/removal
- Chimney capping/covering
- Roof making-good where the chimney is removed
- Gable/flank wall masonry repairs
- Wall ties
- Purlin bearing/support
- Timber inspection and timber repairs
- Rainwater goods, guttering, downpipes and drainage corrections
- VAT or no-VAT status
- Exclusions, assumptions and provisional sums

### Structural Responsibility

> Can you confirm whether Roofcraft would act as principal contractor for the whole job, including the structural masonry, wall ties and purlin support?

Ask:

- Which structural elements would Roofcraft do directly?
- Which structural elements would be subcontracted?
- Who would choose, manage and pay any subcontractors?
- Who would be responsible if subcontracted structural work failed or needed snagging?
- Will the work follow the White & Lloyd structural recommendations?
- Do you need any further structural engineer input before starting?
- Should Building Control be involved?
- Is planning permission or party-wall input needed for chimney reduction/removal?

### Roof Replacement Recommendation

> You recommended complete replacement rather than repair/overhaul. Could you set out the main reasons for that in the quote or by email?

Ask:

- What specific defects make repair/overhaul poor value?
- Is there evidence of widespread nail sickness?
- Are the battens likely to be unsound?
- Are the existing slates too fragile, delaminated or inconsistent to reuse?
- Is any local repair still needed before the main job starts?
- If the roof is stripped, will you photograph timber, batten and slate condition before covering anything back up?

### Slate / Tile Choice

> Could you quote natural slate and concrete tile separately, with exact product details, and comment on weight/structural implications?

Ask:

- What exact natural slate product would you use?
- What exact concrete tile product would you use?
- What are the weights of each option per square metre?
- Given the existing purlin/gable issues, is concrete structurally appropriate?
- Should White & Lloyd confirm the roof can take concrete tiles before I choose that option?
- Would fibre-cement slate be an option, or would you not recommend it here?
- Which option is best for long-term value, appearance and future solar mounting?

### Solar Coordination

> If I am likely to install solar later, what should be planned during the reroof so the new roof is not disturbed unnecessarily?

Ask:

- Can solar mounting positions be planned before the roof is installed?
- Would any roof covering choice make solar easier or harder?
- Can cable routes or roof penetrations be allowed for?
- Would you want a solar installer involved before roofing starts?
- Can scaffold be left available for solar installation if timing works?

### Guarantees / Insurance

> Could you send details of your insurance and the guarantee that would apply to this specific job?

Ask for:

- Public liability insurance certificate
- Employer's liability insurance, if applicable
- Cover level and expiry date
- Whether the workmanship guarantee is from Roofcraft only or insurance-backed
- Whether a CORC insurance-backed guarantee is available
- Whether TrustATrader or Checkatrade guarantees apply and must be registered before work starts
- Separate guarantee terms for roof covering, leadwork, chimney work, structural masonry, wall ties and purlin support
- Manufacturer/material warranties for slate/tile, membrane and other products

### References / Previous Work

> Could you send the details of the previous customers/projects you mentioned, including the nearby reroof if possible?

Ask for:

- At least two recent full reroof references
- One reference from an older house if available
- One reference where chimney or structural work was involved if available
- Address or road name for the nearby comparable reroof, if the homeowner is comfortable with that
- Permission to speak to previous customers directly

Questions for references:

- Did the final cost match the quote?
- Were variations explained and agreed before work proceeded?
- Did the job start and finish when expected?
- Was the site kept tidy?
- Were neighbours handled well?
- Were photos provided during the work?
- Were any leaks or defects found afterwards?
- How was snagging handled?
- Would you use Ben/Roofcraft again?

### Payment Terms

> Could you set out the proposed payment schedule and what each stage payment relates to?

Check:

- Avoid large upfront payments.
- Prefer staged payments linked to scaffold/material delivery, watertight stage, completion and snagging.
- Confirm whether any deposit is required and why.
- Confirm who owns materials delivered to site before installation.
- Confirm whether the quote is fixed price or estimate.
- Confirm how variations are priced and approved.
- Confirm start date, duration and working hours.
- Confirm how weather delays are handled.
