# 2026-09-08 Leak Discovery And Roofcraft Inspection

## Summary

Active roof leaks were discovered while loft ladder work was underway. Roofcraft / Ben attended for a free same-day inspection, recommended complete roof replacement, and became the current leading contractor candidate.

## Roof Condition Evidence

While attempting to install a loft ladder on 08/09/2026, improved visibility into the loft revealed several visible gaps/holes in the slate roof where daylight could be seen.

It was raining at the time and active water ingress was confirmed, with intermittent drips entering the loft.

The water ingress was not flooding or a substantial flow, but it demonstrated that the existing slate covering is no longer fully watertight.

Important known facts:

- Original approximately 1900s slate roof
- Approximately 120 years old
- No roofing underlay
- Visible gaps/daylight through roof
- Confirmed intermittent rainwater ingress
- Roofcraft inspected the roof in person
- Complete roof replacement became a serious/leading option

## Roofcraft Contact History

Roofcraft was originally approached solely for an urgent inspection of the active roof leak.

Initial WhatsApp message explained:

- Active leak through old slate roof
- Rainwater dripping into loft
- Visible gaps through roof from loft
- Request for inspection and watertight repair

Roofcraft replied requesting:

- Name
- Property address
- Contact number
- Brief description
- Optional photos

Benjamin then offered a same-day site visit. The inspection/quotation visit was confirmed as free of charge.

## Site Visit

Ben attended the property and made a very good impression.

Stephen found him knowledgeable, helpful and easy to deal with.

Key points from the visit:

- Roofcraft can potentially handle both roofing and the structural elements of the project.
- Ben believes Roofcraft can take responsibility for the gable/chimney/purlin-related structural work as well as the roof.
- Ben recommended complete roof replacement rather than continued repair/overhaul.
- Ben strongly recommended against reusing the existing slates.
- He will provide formal quotations.
- He gave an approximate two-week lead time.
- Indicative job duration for the roof was approximately five days.
- Roofcraft is willing to provide a free patch to the current leak pending roof replacement.
- Ben is going to provide details/references for previous roof-replacement customers.
- One previous Roofcraft reroof is apparently one road away, making it potentially possible to inspect a comparable local job.

## Roof Replacement Ballpark

Ben gave the following verbal ballpark only:

- New natural slate: approximately £20,000
- Concrete tiles: approximately £18,000

Concrete was therefore approximately £2,000 cheaper in Ben's rough verbal estimate.

These are not yet written quotes.

Need to confirm whether these figures include:

- VAT
- Scaffolding
- Waste disposal
- New membrane
- New battens
- Timber repairs
- Leadwork
- Ridge work
- Chimney-related roof making-good
- Gutters/rainwater goods
- Structural work

Do not assume any of these are included until the written quotation arrives.

## Solar Coordination

Stephen and Ben discussed coordinating the reroof with future solar installation.

Ben said this would not be a problem.

This creates a useful opportunity to coordinate solar panel layout, mounting requirements, cable routes, roof penetrations, scaffold access and roof covering choice.

If solar is likely in the near term, obtain a solar design/quote before roof work starts if practical.
